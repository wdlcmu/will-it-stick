A friend asked what was new. Rather than say “good, busy,” he let three cold readers judge his own line.

What they heard

“The author starts basketball on leave.”

The sport stuck, but no scene came with it. Stories scored 1 of 10.

The one fix

Say, “I am playing basketball during my time off.”
