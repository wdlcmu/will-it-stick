Privacy

Your words are private to you.

When you run the panel, your text is sent once to the AI readers, scored, and the result comes back to your screen.

We store nothing. There is no database of your words, no account, and no log of your text.

The only numbers we keep are run counts and cost, so the demo stays within its budget.

Closing the page closes the run. Nothing follows you.
