Privacy

Your words are private to you.

When you run the panel, your text is sent once to the AI readers, scored, and the result comes back to your screen.

Your words are not recorded or kept. There is no database of your words. There is no account, and there are no saved checks.

Each day, the server keeps one count: how many checks ran. The count is integers only, with no text. It keeps the demo within its budget.

Closing the page closes the run. Nothing follows you.
