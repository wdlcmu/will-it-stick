What makes an idea stick?

Six checks. One plain line each. Ideas that pass get repeated.

The six checks

Simple. One core, stated first. “Start with a name, not a number.”

Unexpected. A break in the pattern. “The meeting is cancelled — forever.”

Concrete. Things you can touch. “A red door,” not “an entrance.”

Credible. Proof a skeptic can check: a source, a test, a number with its source named.

Emotional. One person, not a crowd. “Marta waits two hours for the bus.”

Stories. A small scene with a turn. Someone tries, something changes.

A real check

From a real panel run. The full run is on Examples.

Before

I am starting basketball on my leave of work

After

I am playing basketball on my leave of work.

What readers heard

The core: “The author starts basketball on leave.” The fix said “during my time off”; the rewrite changed one word only, starting became playing.
