How to make a message stick

Say one thing. Say it in things you can touch. Leave one gap. End with a scene.

The four moves

1. One core. If they can only keep one line, which line?

Pick it before you write the rest.

2. Concrete words. Swap each big word for a thing, a place, or a time.

3. One gap. Open a question your reader wants closed.

Answer it one beat later than they expect.

4. One scene. End on a moment they can picture, not a summary.

Watch it happen

This pair is written for this page. It is not a panel run.

Before

Team, going forward we all need to communicate better and stay aligned on priorities.

After

Every Friday at four, one of us reads the week's worst email out loud. Then we fix it together.

What changed

“Communicate better” became a time, a place, and an act.

There is now one line a listener can repeat on Monday.
