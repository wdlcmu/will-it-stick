A repair night note

Ask or invite

The site's own sample text.

The words

Your toaster died on a Tuesday. The shop wants $90 to look at it. We fix it free. Every first Saturday, neighbors bring broken lamps, bikes, and toasters to the Maple Street hall. Last month we fixed 63 things and threw away none of them. Bring one broken thing. Leave with it working, plus a cup of coffee.

The goal

Get neighbors to come to one repair night.

The core they heard

“Fix things free.”
