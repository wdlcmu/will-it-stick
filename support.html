Common questions

Is it really free? Yes. No account, no signup, no catch.

Do you keep my words? No. Your words are not recorded or kept. The server keeps only a daily count of how many checks ran.

The readers heard a point I didn't make. Email us the draft and the result. That feedback is how the readers get better.

The winning version changed my meaning. Send it to us. A version that changes your facts should never win, and we want to know.

Privacy

How we handle data is on the privacy page. Short version: your text runs through the panel and is gone. Nothing is kept but the daily count.
